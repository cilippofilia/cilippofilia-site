Liquid Glass

Glass is for functional chrome: the header, floating cards, buttons standing on the page. It never stacks: a control on a glass card gets a flat tint, not a second blur. The ambient blooms behind the page are what the glass refracts.

--glass-fill
--glass-fill-strong
--glass-border
--glass-highlight
--glass-blur
--glass-shadow
--glass-shadow-hover
--ease-spring

--glass-fill-strong is the fallback when a browser can't blur or the visitor asks for reduced transparency. --ease-spring is the one curve for anything that moves on hover, press or scroll.

Radius

--radius-lg is every floating glass surface, --radius a surface nested one level inside one, and --radius-pill anything fully rounded. App icons use --radius-icon, a share of the tile's size, so every icon rounds off the same.

Typography
