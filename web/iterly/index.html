Iterly

Iterly

A lightweight project tracker for indie developers. Plan by release, track by task, and watch your momentum build.

Download on the App Store View on GitHub

Free on iPhone. Local-first, no account, nothing to sign up for.

Three tabs

See it, plan it, ship it

  • 🏠DashboardPin up to four projects and see what's due next. Upcoming tasks are ordered by due date and priority, so the next thing to do is always at the top.
  • 📦ProjectsEvery project carries its status, priority, notes and the release you're working towards. Tasks and subtasks live underneath, grouped by what's shipping next.
  • 🔥ActivityA GitHub-style heatmap of your progress over the last three, six or twelve months. Tap any day to see exactly what moved.
Also in the box

The small things

  • 🏷️Release-centricGive each project a current version and pull its live version number straight from the App Store by ID.
  • ✅Tasks and subtasksStatus, priority, due date, notes and details on each one. Blocked, not started, in progress, done.
  • 📎AttachmentsAdd screenshots and screen recordings to a task, so the bug you're fixing is right there next to it.
  • 🔗Useful linksKeep GitHub, GitLab, Figma, TestFlight, website and custom links on each release, one tap away.
  • 💡BrainstormA scratchpad on every project for ideas that aren't tasks yet.
  • 📱WidgetsSmall, medium and large Home Screen widgets with your heatmap and streak flame. Choose which projects the large one shows.
Momentum

Keep the streak alive

Log progress every day and the streak flame on your widget burns brighter. Skip a day and it cools, gradually, as a gentle nudge to get back to it. No notifications, no guilt trips. Just a flame that tells the truth.
