Better cocktails at home: 100+ classic recipes, bartending lessons, and a cabinet that knows what you already have.

Download on the App Store View on GitHub

Free on iPhone, iPad and Mac. No account required.

Three tabs

Learn it, mix it, stock it

  • 📖LearnLessons on bar basics, prep, spirits, liqueurs and syrups, plus a reading list and calculators for ABV and super juice.
  • 🍸Cocktails100+ cocktails and shots with ingredients, method, history and related drinks. Search, sort by glass or ice, and filter to favourites.
  • 🗄️CabinetSort the bottles you own into categories, star the ones you reach for, and check what you have before you shop.
Also in the box

The small things

  • 📅Cocktail of the DayA home screen widget with a fresh pick every day. Choose all drinks, cocktails, shots or your favourites.
  • ✍️Your own recipesAdd the drinks you invent and keep them next to the classics.
  • ⚖️Ounces or millilitresEvery recipe converts with one tap, whichever jigger you own.
  • ☁️iCloud syncYour cabinet and custom cocktails follow you between iPhone, iPad and Mac.
  • 🌍Five languagesEnglish, Italian, French, German and Spanish.
  • 💻Mac tooThe same recipes and lessons on the desktop, with a native Mac layout.
